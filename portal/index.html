About Luminara

What is this?

Luminara is a 27-card system for counsel and reflection. It was prepared for the Royal Government of Bhutan as part of Project 108 at Gelephu Mindfulness City.

The deck has exactly 27 cards — not 26, not 28. Nobody chose that number: it follows from the simplest possible question applied twice. Three motions, across three depths. Three raised to three.

What it is not

Luminara does not predict the future. It does not assign meanings to dates. A date cannot seed a cast — the system actively prevents this. There is no auspicious hour and no forbidden one.

No card is better or worse than another. Nothing here connects to a network. The portal works entirely offline — the same file on a USB stick, anywhere.

Transparency

Every card's figure (the mandala, the knot) is computed from the card's mathematics, not drawn by hand. No illustration was made. The code is open: everything it does is visible here in this file.
The System

Three Motions

Ask the smallest possible question. What can a thing do?

●Stillholds, remains, does not move —Flowingextends outward, gives ~Turningdraws inward, reflects

There is no fourth. Oscillation is turning named twice; acceleration is the flowing of flow. Every candidate collapses to one of the three.

Three Depths

Draw any boundary and the world divides into exactly three regions: inside, outside, and the line where they meet.

·Fieldthe world — heaviest weight ·Relationthe between — medium weight ·Corethe heart — lightest weight

Three motions × three depths = 27. The number closed itself.

The Three Houses

AUM · bronze · what could be MA · silver · what cuts RA · gold · what radiates

A card's house is set by its heaviest digit (the Field motion). AUM cards hold still in the world; MA cards move outward; RA cards turn inward.

The Law (q)

Each card has a number q in balanced ternary: q = 9·s₀ + 3·s₁ + s₂, where each s ∈ {−1, 0, +1}. This maps every card to a unique integer from −13 to +13. The Seed sits at zero. The Scar at +13. The Return at −13.

The Voice (p)

p counts the moving depths of a card. One more than the number of non-still positions — and when all three depths move, the windings redouble: p = 7.

The Four Silences

Four of the 27 cards do not answer. They ask instead. When you draw a Silence, the card poses a question and waits. The four Silences are: descent (4), widening (16), surrender (22), recognition (25).

A reading that never fell silent would be lying four cells in twenty-seven. The Light of Luminara, XI

Answer & Becoming

Every card has an answer: its antipodal card through the cube's centre, found by negating each digit. Applying the answer twice returns to the original.

Becoming is where a card settles: every turning depth resolves to still. Cards with no turning depths have no slope — they are already still.

The Figure

Each card's artwork is a torus knot computed from p and q. The mandala (from above) has |q| petals. The knot (from the side) winds p times one way and |q| the other. When p and |q| share a factor, the strand closes early and the figure becomes a link — several rings bound together.

How to Cast

The Seed

Every cast is seeded by a string. The same seed always returns the same card — forever. This is intentional: a cast is declared and replayable. Anyone with the seed can verify the draw.

Leave the field empty and a seed will be generated from cryptographic randomness and declared in full at the bottom of your reading.

You may paste any public randomness value (a beacon hash, a shared phrase) as the seed. Nothing here fetches anything — all seeding is done by you.

Cast One

Draws a single card. Use this for a moment of direct reflection — one position, one question, one answer (or one silence).

Cast Three

Draws three cards into three fixed positions using Fisher–Yates shuffle (no replacement): root (what is knotted in and cannot be undone), present (the live cut of the moment), and becoming (what draws forward).

The Guard

A date cannot seed a cast. The system throws by name if a Date object is passed. There is no auspicious hour and no forbidden one — Luminara does not do electional work. That office belongs to those who hold it.

The moment a calendar exists, the electional question knocks: which hour is auspicious? … There is no auspicious hour and no forbidden one. The Astrolabe of the 27, IX
