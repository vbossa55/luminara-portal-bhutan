# THE PRESENTATION — what to say, and in what order

*Speaker notes for the Luminara portal, prepared for the Royal Government of Bhutan.
The portal is the visual; this is the voice beside it.*

---

## Before anything: the four decisions this presentation rests on

Settled 2026-07-30 with the architect.

1. **No Bhutan-tuned edition.** Luminara is presented as it is. The canon already forbids
   blending — *"the method transfers, the vocabularies never"* (Astrolabe §III), the interference
   principle that set the I Ching down entirely (D4), and *"convergence is evidence, never
   authority"* (Light VII). The refusal predates the audience, which is exactly what makes it
   worth something.
2. **The electional boundary is said out loud, early.** Approved as-is.
3. **The Carry travels as arithmetic only.** No reference to karma, none to older accounts, no
   claim to succeed anything. In practice it barely appears in v1 at all.
4. **The framing memo stays an external document**, outside the canon. It claims nothing and
   assigns nothing.

## What must not appear

- The **sealed letters**. Withheld pending the keeper's key; nothing depends on them.
- The word **karma**, and any framing of the Carry as a successor to it. *(Pinned: a test fails
  if `karma` appears in the built portal.)*
- The **map-room lineage shelf** — Tree of Life, Hermetic Principles, Language of the Birds, the
  cypherpunk texts. Each is held at arm's length correctly on its own; shown together to a
  Buddhist state they read as a syncretic cabinet their cosmology is being added to.
- The phrase **"fortune telling"**, and the verb **"predict"**. Use *counsel, reading, mirror,
  weather, bearing*.
- Any **borrowed iconography**. No dharma wheel, no deities, no thangka pastiche. The deck's own
  bronze, silver and gold, and its own derived figures. The absence is legible as respect, and it
  is legible instantly.

## Movement one · Three motions, and there is no fourth

Open with the smallest question, not with the product. *What can a thing do?* It can hold, it can
extend, it can turn.

Test the closure out loud, because that is what makes it a finding rather than a preference:
oscillation is turning named twice; acceleration is the flowing of flow; spiralling is flow and
turning worn together. Nothing is left over.

Then the depths, and this argument is the strong one: draw any boundary at all and the page falls
into exactly three regions — inside, outside, and the line where they meet. So the three depths are
not a modelling choice; they are what a distinction makes.

Land on: **3 × 3 × 3 = 27. Nobody chose the size of this deck.**

> Do not mention the gyukar here. Not yet. If someone in the room notices, let them notice.

## Movement two · A card is a coordinate, not a picture

This is the anti-trespass movement, and it should be delivered as such — but as an argument about
decks, never as reassurance about intentions.

The line to read aloud: *a deck that means by reference dies with its tradition; found in a ruin by
a people who never knew it, a painted deck is mute, its figures asking for a culture that is gone.
This deck, found in the same ruin, reconstructs itself.*

Then the consequence, which is the whole reason this system can cross a border without taking
anything: **there is no table of twenty-seven meanings in the source, because there is nowhere for
one to live.** The test suite forbids per-card data. There is no place in the instrument to put a
borrowed correspondence, and so none is borrowed. This is checkable, not promised — offer the
repository.

## Movement three · The law, and the cube

`q = 9·s₀ + 3·s₁ + s₂`, each s in {−1, 0, +1}. Every whole number from −13 to +13, exactly once.
Balanced ternary. **Collision-free by the numeral system, not by tuning** — that clause matters to
this audience more than the equation does.

Then the cube: 27 cells, the Seed at the centre, and every card's answer is its antipode through
that centre. Thirteen mirrored pairs around one self-mirrored still point, and a card met by its
answer sums to nought. The number line is the cube's shadow.

Then the census, and present it as counted rather than declared: one circle, two coils, eighteen
knots of which four are trefoils, six link-cards in three bound pairs — **and the three pairs turn
out to be answer-pairs, which nobody arranged.**

**Place the phi episode here.** The golden ratio was the system's most flattering claim about
itself. It was put on trial with the measure declared in advance, and it lost — twenty-ninth out of
forty. Root two is the skeleton, forced; the golden ratio is the dress, chosen. Both were kept and
named for what they are.

If there is time, the Astrolabe sequence §XI→XII→XIII→XIV: a claim, its overturning, its
remeasurement, and a rule earned — all four left standing, *because how a finding was reached is
part of the finding.* For a GovTech audience this is scientific integrity; for a monastic reader it
is honesty. It is the same slide, and it is the reason to trust everything else in the room.

## Movement four · Every card sounds

Two windings are two frequencies; a ratio is an interval. Not a metaphor laid over the geometry —
the same fact read in another sense.

The Resonance is a perfect fifth. The Bridge and The Beacon are both the tritone in opposite hands.
The Scar is thirteen against seven, and **it is not called the far dissonance as a judgement; that
is what the numbers do.**

Then the geography: nearness to the Seed is consonance, distance is dissonance, and every becoming
is a cadence toward consonance. The deck's final cadence — the most dissonant interval closing all
the way to silence — was found, not written.

## Movement five · What it refuses

The three refusals: describes and never operates · trajectory and never prediction · beside you and
never above you.

Then the nine names, each with a pin that fails the build. **Lead with COMPANIONSHIP: a companion,
never a guide of souls.** For this room that is the important one — it is a formal renunciation of
the lama's office, written into the build rather than into a promise.

Then the sentence that makes it more than a promise: *misusing the instrument requires changing the
code, not merely the intention. It cannot quietly begin predicting, because there is nothing to
predict with.*

Then the Silences — four of twenty-seven refuse to be read, and all four hold a still core.
*A reading that never fell silent would be lying four cells in twenty-seven.* An oracle that marks
its own edge from the inside.

## Movement six · One card, drawn

Do it live. Cast, and walk the card: code, house, law, voice, form, sound, answer, slope, standing.
Nine readings, every one checkable with a pencil, nothing consulted but the card.

Say the discipline as you do it: the seed is declared before any meaning is spoken, it is printed in
full so anyone may replay the draw and receive the same card, and there is no second draw. If a
public randomness value is available, paste it — and note that **nothing fetches it**, because a
reading surface that reached the network would break two of the nine names at once.

If a Silence lands: do not interpret it. Say what it asks and stop. That will teach more in ten
seconds than the previous five movements.

## Movement seven · What is not being asked for

**The electional boundary, first and plainly.** Read Astrolabe §IX aloud: *there is no auspicious
hour and no forbidden one.* Then say it in your own words:

> Luminara does not do what your astrologers do. It is built so that it cannot. It names no
> auspicious hour and no forbidden one; it only says what o'clock it is on its own map. Your
> calendar keeps the auspicious hours — that office is not ours, and we are not applying for it.

Then demonstrate it, because it is demonstrable: `castOne(new Date())` throws, by name, with that
sentence in the error message. An instrument that structurally refuses the electional question
cannot encroach on the institution whose office that is. **Non-competing by construction.**

**Then the 27, said and immediately deflated.** The moon's sidereal lap is 27.32 days, which is why
lunar-station systems across several civilisations counted twenty-seven or twenty-eight. The
agreement is the moon's, not ours — any system giving the lunar month daily stations is forced to
that number. Arithmetic, not a marvel. Saying the deflating half yourself is what makes it land.

**Then the sealed room, and the invitation.** One coordinate is withheld: which letter seats where
is held by its keeper until the day it is confirmed. Nothing depends on it. *A book that could not
fail its own last test would not be worth the tests it passed.*

Close on the posture, not on an ask: this is not a finished truth brought for endorsement. It is an
instrument with a falsification still pending, brought to readers whose judgement of the structure
would be genuinely valued — with no claim on anyone's vocabulary, anyone's calendar, or anyone's
office.

## If asked

**"Is this divination?"** It is an instrument for reflection with a divinatory form and no
divinatory claims. It describes and never operates; it offers trajectory and never prediction; it
issues no verdict on any person. Where it cannot honestly speak, it says so — four cells in
twenty-seven.

**"Where do the answers come from?"** From arithmetic and from chance, declared and replayable.
Nowhere else, and it is important to us to say that plainly rather than let a grander answer be
assumed.

**"Does it agree with our astrology?"** On one number, and the number belongs to the moon rather
than to either of us. Past that, the honest answer is that we have not looked and would not
presume to; the canon forbids importing another system's meanings, and that rule was written before
this meeting existed.

**"Could you make a version for Bhutan?"** Not by blending the systems — that would break the law
this instrument is built on, and an instrument that broke its own law to please us would be worth
nothing to you. What could be built, if it were ever wanted, is something co-authored, on your
terms, with your people holding the parts that are theirs to hold. That is a later conversation and
it is yours to open, not ours.

**"What do you want?"** A reading of the structure by people qualified to judge it. Nothing more at
this stage.
